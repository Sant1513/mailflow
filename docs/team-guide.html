MailFlow, from first sign-in to first reply

Email students and recruiters from your own Gmail, and collect e-signatures, in one place. Import a list, write one email, check it per person, send, and see every reply threaded back to the person it came from.

Sign in with your Masai Google account Sends from your own Gmail address Every bulk email has an Unsubscribe button

01Day-one setup

Do these in order. It takes about five minutes.

  1. Sign in at mailflowbymasai.vercel.app with Continue with Google. The first time, your account waits until a Super Admin approves it (Approvals → User Registrations).
  2. Connect Gmail: Settings → Gmail → Connect, and approve the permissions. MailFlow sends from your own address, never as anyone else.
  3. Add your email signature in Settings → Email signature. Quick Send and Recruiter Bulk Send can add it for you.
  4. Optional, in Settings → Gmail: Allow my mailbox as a backup sender (helps teammates when their mailbox hits Google's daily limit) and profile photos for your contacts.
  5. Check your role in Settings → Profile. If it is wrong, a Super Admin changes it.
  6. Send yourself a test: Templates → any template → Send test email, or use Send a test to me in Recruiter Bulk Send.
Find anything fast. Ctrl K opens the command menu. Press ? for keyboard shortcuts: g i Inbox, g c Campaigns, g d Dashboard, g s Settings, g m switch to Mail, g e switch to Sign. The theme switch offers System, Light or Dark.

02Mail, Sign and roles

MailFlow has two products. Switch between them from the product switcher at the top of the sidebar.

  • Mail: Quick Send, Dashboard, Inbox, Data, Contacts, Campaigns, Templates, PDF Library, Automations, Batches, Suppressions, Deliverability, Performance, Reports, History.
  • Sign: Documents, New Request, Bulk Send, Templates, Analytics, PDF Library.
RoleCan do
ViewerRead everything in their workspace. Cannot edit, send or reply.
OperatorImport data, build templates and campaigns, run dry runs, submit campaigns and Quick Sends for approval, reply in the Inbox, run automations, send documents for signature.
AdminEverything an Operator can, plus: approve or reject other people's requests, Send without approval, Quick Send without approval, Recruiter Bulk Send and Mailbox usage. Cannot approve their own request.
Super AdminEverything, organisation-wide: Organization (with the Everything sent ledger), Users, Workspaces, All Data, All Conversations, Audit Logs, System Settings, user registrations, and read-only, audited View workspace as.

Every person has their own workspace: their data, templates, campaigns and inbox. Nobody else's workspace is visible unless they share their inbox with you or a Super Admin opens it with View As.

03Data and contacts

3.1 Import a list Data

  • + Dataset, or Import → paste from Google Sheets or Excel (include the header row), or upload CSV / XLSX.
  • The preview detects headers and column types and flags duplicate emails: Keep first, Keep latest, or Import all.
  • Set the email column's type to EMAIL. That makes the dataset sendable and links each row to a Contact.
Importing never sends anything. Neither do previews, dry runs or AI suggestions.

3.2 Work the grid

  • Double-click a cell to edit. Click a header to sort. Filter builds conditions (equals, contains, is empty…), Group by counts per value, Search matches every column, and Save view keeps a filter for everyone using the dataset.
  • Bulk edit: tick rows → Set column… to value → Apply.
  • Large lists are paged. Every list in MailFlow shows Showing x–y of N with the full total and lets you jump to a page.

3.3 Contacts and segments Contacts

  • Contacts is everyone you have emailed or who wrote to you, with a timeline of every email, reply, note and status change.
  • Fill missing names adds names to contacts that have none, from your recipient lists and their own replies. It never overwrites a name.
  • Profile photos appear once you turn them on in Settings → Gmail (read-only access to your Google contacts).
  • Segments (Contacts → Segments) save a group of contacts by rule. Quick Send can send to a segment.

04Templates and campaigns

4.1 Write a template Templates

  • Subject and body use the list's columns as variables: {{Name}}, {{Deadline}}. Insert them from + Insert variable so the spelling matches.
  • Preview as a real person shows the filled-in email; anything unfilled is listed in yellow.
  • The AI assistant can draft, shorten, fix grammar or translate. Run health check catches missing variables, broken links and a missing mailbox.
  • Every save is a new version. A campaign keeps the version it was created with, so editing a template later never changes an email already sent.

4.2 Build and send a campaign Campaigns

  1. + Campaign: name, dataset, template.
  2. Sender settings: From name, Reply-To, CC / BCC (they go on every email). Add PDF attachments or pick them from the PDF Library, and optional RSVP buttons ("I'll attend / Can't make it") whose answers are saved for you.
  3. Backup mailboxes: tick teammates' mailboxes that offered themselves as backups. They are used only if your mailbox reaches Google's daily limit, is slowed down or is disconnected. Replies still come back to you and the campaign.
  4. Run simulation: exactly who would receive it and who is skipped and why (invalid, duplicate, unsubscribed, bounced). Nothing is sent.
  5. Review the rendered email for at least three people.
  6. Submit for approval, then Send now once approved (or schedule it). Admins can Send without approval.

4.3 Approve Admins

  • Approvals in the sidebar lists campaigns and Quick Sends waiting for a decision, with a total and paging. Super Admins also see User Registrations there.
  • Approve takes optional remarks; Reject needs a reason, which the requester sees. The decision is emailed back in the same thread. You cannot approve your own request.

4.4 Watch the send Batches

  • Every send has live progress: sent, failed, skipped, still queued. Pause, Resume, Cancel, Retry failed and Process queue act immediately.
  • Emails leave at about 20 per minute per mailbox, so 300 people take about 15 minutes.

4.5 When someone replies Reply rules

  • On a campaign, When someone replies lets you react by reply type: Interested, Not interested, Asks a question, Asks for something, Complains, Needs action, Done what was asked, Just says thanks, Out of office.
  • For each type: reply with a template (as a draft for approval, or automatically in the same thread), don't reply, assign to someone, or write a value into a data column. Needs AI to be switched on.

4.6 Automations Automations

  • An automation sends a template when a record matches conditions, with a cooldown so nobody is emailed twice by mistake. Stop conditions such as Reply Received = true always win.
  • Enabling shows how many records it would affect right now and asks you to confirm. Every run is listed with a "Why?" explanation.

05Quick Send

For a one-off email to a list without building a dataset and template first. Every email is personalised and tracked (opens, clicks, replies) and has an unsubscribe link.

  1. Who gets it: paste one email per line, or Name, email per line, or copy rows straight from Sheets / Excel with the header row. You can also choose a list from Data or a segment.
  2. The email: write it, or start from a template. Use {{Name}} and other column names. Add PDFs (up to 4 MB each, 10 MB in total) or pick from the PDF Library. Optional RSVP buttons.
  3. More options: sender name, Reply-To, Cc / Bcc, send later, and backup mailboxes.
  4. Ready to send? shows a check and a preview of the real email as you type. Then send.
Admins send straight away. Operators' Quick Sends go to an admin for approval and send by themselves once approved; you get an email when that happens. Your recent Quick Sends shows progress, updating every few seconds.

06Recruiter Bulk Send Admins

For emailing many recruiters at once, from one or several mailboxes. The page walks through seven steps; Open all steps shows them together.

  1. Recruiters: paste a sheet with a header row, upload CSV / Excel, or choose saved data. Choose the email address column. The other columns appear as chips you can use in the email.
  2. How it goes out:
    • BCC groups (up to 250 recruiters per email, with a visible "To" address), or Individual copies (one email per recruiter).
    • The pause between emails from the same mailbox, in seconds.
    • With more than one mailbox: All mailboxes together (the default; they send in rounds, side by side) or One mailbox after another.
  3. The email: subject, Simple or HTML, and Personalise chips. Variables look like {{Company}}, with a fallback for empty cells: {{Name | there}}. For a per-recruiter tracking link, put the link in a column (for example UTM link) and use href="{{UTM link}}". Links are sent exactly as written. Also From name, CC (up to 10), Reply-To, and Add my email signature.
  4. Attachments and video: up to 5 PDFs from your computer or the PDF Library, and a video attached (up to 20 MB) or shared as a link, which shows a "▶ Watch the video" button. Share Drive videos as "Anyone with the link".
  5. Send from: choose one or more mailboxes, yours and teammates' backups, with each one's usage today and its limit.
  6. Batches and who receives it: a summary of every batch and which mailbox sends it. Click a recruiter to see exactly the email they will get; use ‹ › or ↑ ↓ to move through them.
  7. Check and send: Send a test to me first, then send now or schedule it.
  • Variables need Individual copies. A BCC email is one email for many people, so it cannot say each recruiter's name.
  • A variable that is not a column blocks the send. Recruiters whose cell is empty (with no fallback) are skipped, and the review says so.
  • Reply-To always includes the sending mailbox, so MailFlow still tracks every reply even if you add your own Reply-To. With individual copies, every CC address gets one copy per recruiter, so CC works only for up to 50 recruiters; for more, use BCC groups or remove the CC.
  • While it runs: Pause, Resume, Cancel. Each mailbox has its own pause and limit. The progress list shows every recruiter; click one to see the email they got. You can copy a past send to start a new one.
  • Replies land in the Inbox of the admin who sent it, assigned to them, whichever mailbox sent the email.

07Inbox and replies

  • Replies to emails MailFlow sent arrive in the Inbox. Gmail is checked automatically every 30 minutes, and again whenever you open the Inbox if it is more than 2 minutes old. Sync now fetches the latest at once.
  • Bounces, out-of-office and auto-replies are recognised and never count as "they replied".
  • In a conversation: reply in the same thread, attach files (up to 4 MB per reply, including signed documents), add an internal note, tag, assign, set a follow-up, schedule a reply, change status. AI suggest reply drafts text you can edit.
  • Saved replies (Settings → Saved replies): reusable answers you insert while replying.
  • Inbox sharing (Settings → Inbox sharing): share your inbox with everyone in the organisation or with specific people, as Can view or Can view and reply.
  • SLA rules (Settings → SLA rules): how fast conversations should get a first reply and be resolved; overdue ones are flagged.
Replies to bounced or spam-reported addresses are blocked. If an address bounced or marked you as spam, MailFlow refuses to send to it, to protect everyone's sending reputation.

08Unsubscribes and suppressions

  • Every bulk email has an Unsubscribe button: campaigns, Quick Send, automations, and Recruiter Bulk Send, including BCC emails.
  • On personal emails (campaigns, Quick Send, automations, individual copies) it is a one-click link.
  • On a BCC email it opens a pre-filled email to the sending mailbox ("Unsubscribe rb-…"). MailFlow reads it, suppresses that person, and skips them for the rest of the send. There is also an "unsubscribe on the web" link.
  • Suppressions lists everyone MailFlow will not email, with the reason (Unsubscribed, Bounced, Marked as spam) and who added it: you, the recipient's unsubscribe link, or the system.
  • Bounces and spam reports apply to the whole organisation and carry an Org-wide badge: if an address bounced for a teammate, it is skipped for you too ("System (teammate's bounce)"). Unsubscribes apply to your workspace.
  • You can add an address by hand (Unsubscribed or Complaint, with a note) or remove one you added.

09Sign (e-signatures)

  • New Request: document name, signer name and email, CC (one per line), custom fields such as {{stipend}}, an optional invitation email, extra attachments, and when the link expires (7 days by default). Or load a template.
  • Bulk Send: one document per row from a CSV or a Data list, with a template. With several signers, choose Signer 1 → 2 → 3 (in order) or all sign at once, and which signer fills which fields.
  • Templates hold reusable documents with fields; the PDF Library holds PDFs shared by Mail and Sign.
  • Documents lists every request and its status. Signers who have not signed get a reminder every 2 days, up to 3 reminders.
  • If an invitation email fails, the document shows a warning with Resend. Bulk void and resend report any that failed; Void all asks first. A voided link tells the signer the sender cancelled it.
  • Analytics: documents sent and signed, sign rate and average time to sign.

10Reports and numbers

10.1 In the app

  • Dashboard: live numbers for your workspace and quick actions.
  • Deliverability: emails sent, opened and clicked (people only), bounce and unsubscribe rates against healthy levels, domain authentication (SPF, DKIM, DMARC), each mailbox in the last 24 hours, and recent bounces. Last 7, 30 or 90 days.
  • Performance: the team's numbers per person. Reports: documents sent and signed, emails sent, open and click rates, conversations and resolution rate.
  • Date ranges are in IST and include today.
  • History: every email sent (with its outcome and a "Why?") and every message received.

10.2 The daily report 8 PM IST

  • Sent at 8 PM IST, Monday to Friday, covering 8 PM the previous working day to 8 PM today. Work done after 8 PM goes into the next report, and the weekend goes into Monday's report. A "What this report covers" card at the top says exactly which hours.
  • Contents: totals; sends by type and by person; who used whose mailbox; the inbox per person (received, pending, closed, first and average response time, today and the last 30 days); and what needs attention.
  • It goes to the To and CC people set in System Settings (visible), or to the admins by BCC if none are set, and posts to its own Slack channel as "MailFlow Daily Report".

10.3 The weekly report Monday 9 AM IST

  • Same design, covering last week Monday to Sunday day by day, compared with the week before.
  • Super Admins can Preview latest, Send test to me and Send test to Slack for both reports in System Settings → Reports & triage, with Email and Slack preview tabs.

11Admin and Super Admin

  • Mailbox usage (Admins): each mailbox's last 24 hours against the limits of 1,500 emails and 1,800 different people, so you can pick mailboxes with room.
  • Organization: users, workspaces, emails, replies and campaigns over 7, 30 or 90 days, and the Everything sent ledger. Filter it by period, workspace, who launched it, mailbox, type, status, or only from someone else's mailbox, and download it as CSV. The 7d / 30d / 90d links keep your filters.
  • Users: roles, enable and disable. You cannot disable yourself or remove the last Super Admin.
  • Workspaces: View Workspace opens one read-only under a red banner; entry and exit are audited.
  • All Data, All Conversations, Audit Logs: organisation-wide, and every view is audited.
  • System Settings, with its own section menu: Data retention, Reports & triage (daily report, weekly report, AI inbox triage), Slack, and Runtime.

13Before you send

Tick these off for every bulk send. Ticks are saved in this browser.

14Limits to know

Only these send email: Send now on an approved campaign, Send without approval by an admin, Quick Send, Recruiter Bulk Send, a scheduled send, an inbox reply you send or schedule, an enabled automation, and an automatic reply rule. Nothing else does.
  • Per mailbox, per 24 hours: 1,500 emails and 1,800 different people, so you stay under Google's own limits. Check Mailbox usage. Backup mailboxes take over when yours is full.
  • Speed: about 20 emails per minute per mailbox by default. Recruiter Bulk Send uses the pause you set.
  • Attachments: Quick Send PDFs up to 4 MB each and 10 MB in total; Recruiter Bulk Send up to 5 PDFs and a video up to 20 MB (or a link of any size); inbox replies up to 4 MB.
  • Recruiter Bulk Send: up to 250 recruiters per BCC email; CC up to 10 people.
  • Data grid: up to 100,000 rows per dataset.
  • AI: daily limits per person and per organisation (see System Settings). If AI is unavailable, carry on by hand; nothing else is affected.
